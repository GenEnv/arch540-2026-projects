Designing the Design

Building custom AI tools for architectural workflows

A studio that builds: students design, build and govern working tools that make architectural knowledge operational, each grounded in a real workflow. First a small tool on one Vancouver regulation, then a final project on a workflow of their own choosing. This page collects the work shown at the two reviews so far; each entry is written and kept up to date by the student.

Final project · Mid-review Assignment 1 · Vancouver Regulation Toolkit

Final project: mid-review

October 23, 2026 · final review December 10, 2026

For the final project each student chose a real workflow in architecture, landscape or a neighbouring practice, studied how it is done today and which tools already exist for it, and proposed a tool that AI makes possible. Each entry has the student’s proposal, the repository and the app where one exists, and the presentation from the mid-review.

Assignment 1: Vancouver Regulation Toolkit

Reviewed October 2, 2026 · 15 tools

Assignment 1 asked each student to take one piece of a Vancouver regulation or design guideline, understand it, and build a small interactive tool with AI that explains, computes and visualizes it. Each entry has the student’s own one-sentence brief, a link to the tool, the live demo from the review, and the discussion that followed.
